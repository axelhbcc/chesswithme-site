ChessWithMe

Delete your account

You can delete your ChessWithMe account and everything attached to it at any time. Deletion is permanent — nothing can be restored afterwards.

From the app

  1. Open ChessWithMe and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to the Account section.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and data are removed immediately.

If you no longer have the app

Email us from the address linked to your account:

contactchesswithme@gmail.com

Use the subject line “Account deletion request”. We verify that the request comes from the registered address, then delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: username, avatar, bio, chess level and linked ratings, age, gender, city, preferences
  • Every game and meetup you created
  • Your participation in other players' games and meetups
  • All your chat messages
  • Ratings and reviews you wrote, and ratings and reviews you received
  • Your notification settings and push token
  • Your login credentials

What we keep, and for how long

If another player reported you, we keep a record of that report for 12 months after deletion. This is limited to the report itself and is retained to protect other players who meet in person. It is not linked to a usable profile and is not used for any other purpose.

Emails already sent to you — account confirmation, password reset — remain in our email provider's delivery logs for up to 30 days, after which they are removed automatically.

Deleting your account does not cancel games you host on other players' behalf. If you host an upcoming game or meetup, cancel it first so the other participants are notified.
