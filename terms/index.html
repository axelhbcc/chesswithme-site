ChessWithMe is a directory and matchmaking tool that helps chess players find and organize in-person, physical games with other players. You can create or join a Duel (a 1-on-1 game between two players) or a Meetup (a group session with 3 to 8 players, or an unspecified number, organized by a host).

We are not a chess platform, rating authority, or online chess engine, and we do not organize, supervise, host, staff, or attend any game or meetup.
